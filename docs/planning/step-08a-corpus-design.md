# Step 08A — corpus evidence and human-review design

Date: 2026-10-25, Asia/Kolkata. **Status: proposed design only, within the approved offline Step 08A.** This document defines the future corpus contract and acceptance examples. It creates no schema, database, corpus release, identity merge, human label, partition or test implementation.

The original [Completion Step 08 requirements](../project-completion-plan.md#step-08--deliver-the-full-reviewed-multilingual-corpus) remain in force: approximately 5,000 usable reference groups, 2,000 independent absent-query groups, at least 200 proposed positive and 200 negative candidate reviews, adjudication of every hard negative used, and at least 100 independent final positive groups for each claimed Latin↔Arabic, Latin↔Cyrillic and Latin↔Han direction. These are planning requirements, not achieved counts or statistical guarantees. Shortfalls require more approved work or an explicit scope decision.

The [approved Step 07 record](../progress/completion-07-approved.md) establishes three successful feasibility sources: OpenSanctions UN, OFAC and EU. Direct UN and Wikidata remain investigated but not acquired. Their prior investigation does not authorize future use. In particular, a different Wikidata organization/subclass query needs a new explicit review and authorization. The central [Step 08A plan](../step-08a-plan.md) owns proposed acquisition quantities, budgets and the Step 08B approval request. The [partition and approval gates](step-08a-partition-and-approval-gates.md) own partition allocation, access custody and later scoring restrictions.

## Purpose, inputs and present limits

The goal is a reproducible research dataset in which every name, label and exclusion can be traced to permitted evidence. The dataset assists possible-record-match research; neither a provider listing nor a reviewed name relationship establishes wrongdoing, current legal status or an operational screening conclusion.

Two populations must be documented separately: multilingual non-person names for learning, and fresh permitted sanctions-domain cases for domain evaluation. A supplementary organization corpus cannot substitute for the second population. Provider source, population, sampling method and intended use are explicit fields rather than assumptions inferred from a filename.

The central plan's primary corpus route requires **at least 7,000 disjoint reviewed leakage families: 5,000 fixed-index reference families plus 2,000 controlled index-absence families**. The latter come from permitted non-person pools but are deliberately excluded, with all equivalent copies, from the fixed index. This satisfies the original fixed-index meaning of absence when the evidence, independence and review gates below pass; it is not a claim of natural background prevalence or absence from sanctions lists generally. Natural external nonmembers can be a separately reported supplement. A 200-record API pilot cannot promise 2,000 reviewed absent families. The 13,658 previously observed source-local eligible records would need at least about 51.25% accepted independent-family yield to supply 7,000; that yield is unknown, duplicate counts are unresolved, and the unretained records have not become available merely through this arithmetic.

Existing aggregate evidence describes 600 source-local organization groups, zero vessels, 3,293 name-evidence entries and 3,072 unique strings. It nominates 69 exact-name group pairs for review; it does not confirm 69 duplicates. Broad Latin–Arabic/Cyrillic/Han group-presence counts are 35/96/4, with strict single-script-pair counts 35/88/4. These are exposure and workload facts from the [feasibility report](../../data/eval-comparisons/completion-07/closure-review/feasibility-workload.md), not reviewed identities, validated translations or final labels.

The [new Step 08A offline audit](../../data/eval-comparisons/completion-08a/audit/report.md) finds 386 groups with at least two distinct original names and 214 with only one. Conservative normalization nominates 83 candidate pairs, including the 69 exact pairs and 14 additional cross-source pairs. These measurements enlarge the review queue; they create no identity merges or labels. Multiple original names are potential review material, not automatically correct positives.

Treat all existing 600 groups and their derived aliases conservatively as an exposed development/learning pool. Their actual later learning use still requires reviewed eligibility, labels and purpose permission. They cannot enter fresh calibration-fit, threshold-selection or final-confirmation populations. A source refresh, changed provider ID or additional source copy does not reset exposure for the same identity or dependent family. Aggregate availability does not make the underlying pool fresh merely because a particular name was not displayed in a report.

Mixed bulk response bodies were not retained. A release built from the permitted selections can bind the saved acquisition/body hashes and selection bytes, but cannot claim to replay a discarded raw response locally. Re-fetching, retaining additional fields, training, publishing data and releasing a model each retain their separate approval and permission gates.

## The concepts, in plain English

| Concept: what it means | Why it is needed | When and where it is used | Who establishes it, and how |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Source-qualified record key: the publisher namespace together with its exact record ID. | Two publishers can both use `17` without referring to the same record. | When importing a permitted observation into the corpus ledger. | The importer preserves a structured source/namespace/ID tuple; it never joins records merely because local IDs match. |
| Snapshot: one dated, receipt-bound source selection. Observation: one record as represented in that snapshot. | The same provider record may change, disappear or have its ID reused. | At evidence ingestion, before links or labels. | The importer binds original selection bytes, receipt, parser version and source version or content-hash fallback. |
| Record equivalence: evidence that observations are copies or continuations of the same provider/list record. | Mirrors and provider redirects should not inflate record counts. | During reviewed duplicate resolution. | A reviewer accepts documented referents, redirects or copy lineage under a versioned policy; similar names alone are insufficient. |
| Real-world entity claim: evidence that records refer to the same organization or vessel. | Separate list records may describe one organization; one name may describe several. | When sufficient permitted evidence supports an identity-level research label. | A qualified human records supporting and conflicting evidence and the claim's limits. A provider assertion is preserved as a provider assertion. |
| Leakage family: a conservative set of dependent records, identities and derived examples that must stay together for evaluation separation. | Related copies or examples can leak information even when identity equivalence is unresolved or inappropriate. | Before later partition assignment and whenever a dependency is discovered. | A deterministic grouping rule follows reviewed links and derivation ancestry; humans decide which evidence justifies a dependence link. |
| Quarantine: retained evidence that is excluded from label or evaluation use pending resolution. | Uncertainty must not be converted into a convenient positive or negative. | When links, types, names, permissions or evidence are insufficient or contradictory. | A reviewer or a mechanical validator records a reason and scope; only an evidenced resolution can clear it in a new version. |
| Adjudication: an explicit resolution of a reviewed case, including an unresolved outcome. | Reviewer disagreement and hard negatives need a traceable decision. | After initial review, before a label is used. | A qualified human considers all submissions and evidence; a second independent person is preferred and their actual role is recorded. |
| Fixed index view: an exact versioned set of reference records and allowed names. | A claim that a query is absent or an alias is held out only has meaning against a specified reference set. | When validating corpus query candidates and, later, freezing Step 09 evaluation views. | A view builder enumerates membership and hashes it; reviewers validate meaning and a separate custodian controls final material. |

“Independent group” means the predeclared evaluation unit after reviewed equivalence and conservative dependence grouping. It is not the number of aliases, pairs, source rows or synthetic perturbations. One leakage family can contain several distinct identities; report both counts and use the conservative family count for independence claims.

## Proposed storage model

Use a new corpus store and new versioned manifests. Do not alter the existing source database's application identity/schema or rewrite historical snapshots. The legacy `src/watchlist/ingest/schema.sql` and its loader remain their established contract. The proposed `src/watchlist/corpus_schema.sql` receives its own reviewed application identity and schema version during Step 08B; no numeric identifiers or SQL are allocated here.

Separate an append-only curation ledger from sealed release artifacts. The ledger can receive later review events; a sealed release contains a fixed, validated selection of evidence, link decisions and label decisions. Corrections append a superseding decision and create a new release. “Append-only” and hashes provide reproducibility/integrity checks; they do not make a locally writable disk tamper-proof or prove reviewer independence.

The tables below are a logical proposal. Step 08B should implement them in small slices, first with authored fiction, rather than creating the entire roadmap at once.

A newer rule does not rewrite old evidence. |

AI suggestions are not human annotations. A local username alone does not authenticate or establish independence. |

The corpus contract also reserves `index_views`, view membership, `query_cases`, `exposure_events` and custody-attestation references. A view names its corpus-release hash, exact reference/name membership, transformation policy, exclusions and truth-definition version. A query case names original permitted evidence, derivation ancestry, target truth set or absence construction, review status, direction and view ID/hash. Step 09 owns actual partition assignment and final query/view freezing; Step 08B must not silently create those assignments while building a corpus.

Store identifiers as structured tuples or length-safe canonical encodings. Never obtain a source key by ambiguous string concatenation or by case-folding provider IDs. A name-evidence ID binds the snapshot observation and full evidence tuple, including qualifiers; it is not a hash of name text alone. A later parser correction creates a new observation/derivation version linked to the old one. Logical content hashing uses a declared serialization and stable ordering; physical SQLite page bytes are not the logical identity of a release.

## Equivalence, dependence and uncertain links

Candidate generation may use exact-name equality, provider referents and eventually separately approved matchers. Its output is only a review queue. Retain the method/version and all source-specific evidence so another reviewer can reproduce why a pair was nominated. No matching score is a probability or an identity decision.

Apply these decisions in order:

1. Validate permitted type and provenance. Unknown/ambiguous types, private-person material and evidence outside approved field scope do not enter the eligible non-person pool.
2. Establish record equivalence from explicit, reviewed copy/redirect/continuity evidence. Different source IDs stay separate evidence even when grouped. A reused ID or time conflict prevents automatic continuity.
3. Establish a real-world same-entity claim only when permitted supporting evidence is sufficient. A shared name, address fragment or corporate parent alone is insufficient. Current minimal name-only selections may not support that review; record `needs_permitted_evidence` rather than acquire extra fields or guess.
4. Build conservative leakage families from accepted equivalence/identity and declared example-dependence links. A parent/subsidiary relation does not merge legal identities. If shared branding or derivation creates dependence for the study, keep the records in one family or exclude the ambiguous cases under a predeclared rule.
5. Quarantine unresolved possible duplicates before allocating independent populations. Quarantine covers both endpoints and the affected dependency component, not just the one pair selected for review. Common-name candidate generation alone must not manufacture accepted transitive equivalence.

Review contradictory cycles before computing accepted equivalence closure: if A=B and B=C are accepted but A≠C is evidenced, the component cannot be released as settled. A rejected candidate is not automatically a proven distinct-entity negative. After any later link discovery, recompute counts and exposure; a cross-partition discovery invalidates the affected frozen split/view and requires a new version and review. Never silently move one row while leaving its aliases behind.

## Name evidence and weak-alias quarantine

Keep each original spelling, field path, supplied language and qualifier intact. Script is an observed writing system; it does not prove language, translation, transliteration or identity. Preserve mixed and unknown scripts as explicit states. A reviewer can attach a supported language/relation annotation separately without overwriting the provider field.

An explicitly weak alias starts in quarantine for positive-label generation, negative mining and final query construction. Preserve it as evidence. It may later be used only under a reviewed purpose-specific policy with supporting evidence and a recorded resolution; otherwise it remains excluded and counted. A nonweak alias is still a provider assertion, not an independently correct positive.

Interpret qualifiers by source schema. OFAC's explicitly weak name evidence must remain weak. EU `strong=false` describes an alternative rather than a main name; it must not be reinterpreted as a weak or unreliable alias. Missing strength is `unknown`, not automatically strong. Names with equal text but different language tags, fields or original qualifier values remain distinct evidence entries.

Cross-script pairs require review of both names and their supported relationship. Tag translation, transliteration, spelling variation, abbreviation, historic name and unresolved relation separately. Machine-generated variants carry machine provenance and remain descendants of the same family. A generated spelling does not count as another natural alias, another independent group or another human review.

## Human review protocol

The first review round must include **at least 200 distinct proposed-positive cases and 200 distinct negative-candidate cases**. These are minimum inspected cases, not guaranteed accepted labels. Reviewing both directions of the same pair, repeating a case after feedback, or generating many perturbations does not multiply that minimum. Report candidate cases, distinct endpoint families, submissions, resolved decisions and usable labels separately.

Before exporting cases, freeze a sampling plan with strata for source combination (within-source and cross-source), organization/vessel type, script direction, mixed/unknown script, alias strength and proposed relation. Include easy and confusing negative candidates and exact-name collisions. Allocate review slots from measured availability and the intended claims, including rare-script coverage; record empty strata and shortages explicitly. The present zero-vessel and four-Han-group observations cannot be repaired by calling organization cases vessels, duplicating pairs or inventing translations. Source/script/type overlap is reported without adding overlapping strata as independent people or groups.

Each review packet contains permitted original name/type/provenance evidence, source dates, relevant qualifiers, relation definitions and an `insufficient_evidence` option. It should not reveal an expected answer or, where avoidable, matcher rank/score before the first decision. A hard-negative nomination says “review this confusing candidate,” not “this is definitely different.” Reviewers need suitable language/domain competence or a recorded qualified consultation; an LLM translation is not that consultation.

Use these outcome definitions:

| Review question | Permitted outcome | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Do two name observations support the same research identity/record target under the declared label definition? | `supported_same`, `supported_distinct`, `insufficient_evidence`, `ineligible`. | Only evidence-supported resolved outcomes can be considered for corresponding labels; retain the definition and scope. |
| Is this link a duplicate record, the same entity, a related entity, or unresolved? | Explicit link-kind decision with evidence; uncertainty remains allowed. | Record-equivalence and identity memberships are updated only through reviewed versions. |
| Does a query support more than one eligible target? | A supported target set, or unresolved membership. | A multi-answer truth set is distinct from uncertain identity; do not force a single answer or turn another valid target into a negative. |
| Is a query absent from one fixed view? | Validated absence of the stated construction, supported presence, or unresolved. | Store a view-bound decision, not a claim of being absent from every real-world list. |

Adjudicate every hard negative actually used, including those added after the initial 200-case sample. The adjudication checks both endpoints, accepted and unresolved link closure, evidence sufficiency and the intended use. It must explicitly support a different target under the label definition; failure to select a candidate, a low score, different source IDs or missing equivalence edges is insufficient. Later training-only mining must exclude quarantined or same-family pairs and can never treat unreviewed retrieved candidates as negative training labels.

An initial sample audit does not certify every unreviewed label in a larger corpus. Unreviewed provider alias pairs retain `provider_assertion` origin and are not counted as human positives; using them as weak supervision would require a separate approved policy and separate reporting. All final labels require adequate human evidence. Independent second review and adjudication of final material are preferred wherever feasible, performed within the custodian's access boundary. If a second qualified reviewer is unavailable, record the actual single-review process and limitation, obtain an explicit review-plan decision, and never claim independent double review. If independent final custody is unavailable, the confirmation gate remains unmet; an approval or label cannot make developer-exposed material independent. Neither an assistant nor an automated validator supplies a missing human reviewer or custodian.

Record first-pass disagreement before adjudication, excluded/uncertain counts and reasons, acceptance by stratum, distinct-family counts, reviewer coverage and elapsed review time. Denominators must say whether they count cases, families or submissions. Do not report a label error rate without an appropriate independently reviewed reference standard, or infer deployment prevalence/precision from the deliberately balanced 200/200 review queues. Preserve disagreements and negative findings when decisions are revised.

## Absence and controlled name availability

An absence label is a statement about membership in a particular fixed reference view, under a particular reviewed identity/equivalence relation. It is never “not sanctioned,” “safe,” “not a person of concern,” or absence from all public lists. A low match score or nonmembership in one 200-record selection does not establish the broader claim.

Two constructions must remain separate:

| Construction | Required evidence and mechanics | Counting/interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `all_equivalent_copies_withheld` | From a permitted pool of at least 7,000 reviewed independent families, designate 5,000 fixed-index reference families and 2,000 disjoint controlled-absence families. Exclude every equivalent record/name and conservatively dependent representation of the absence families from the named fixed view; audit the complete membership and identity/non-equivalence decisions. Any unresolved link can invalidate the absence claim. | The primary proposed route to the 2,000 absent-family target. Label and report it explicitly as a controlled index-absence cohort. No family can satisfy both target cohorts; recycling the 5,000 reference families as query-specific removals does not supply another 2,000 independent families. |
| `natural_nonmember` | A separately permitted, adequately identified non-person query family from an external population, with reviewed membership against the complete declared fixed view and its equivalence/dependence closure. Resolve or quarantine potential matches using sufficient permitted evidence. | An optional, separately reported background supplement. Its acceptance requires its own source/evidence review; a future API record or nonmembership in a sampled list is not an automatic label. |

Keep `unresolved_membership` out of positive/absent binary metrics; preserve it as a separately defined ambiguity/review diagnostic. Natural nonmembers and controlled withheld cases receive separate sampling records, counts and metrics. The primary proposed 2,000-family route is controlled exclusion from one fixed reference index, not 2,000 ad hoc indexes with a different member removed for each query. A later mixture with natural background families needs an explicit count/population decision and unchanged nonoverlap, evidence and review rules. No synthetic name, unreviewed source record or missing link fills a shortfall.

Every counted family still needs adequate identity and non-equivalence review; mechanically excluding IDs cannot prove that an unreviewed alternate copy is absent. Review the complete proposed family membership, resolve relevant cross-cohort links and quarantine uncertainty before certifying either target count. The initial 200-positive/200-negative review minimum is a quality-review workload, not a substitute for the eligibility/equivalence work across the full accepted population. Every hard negative actually used retains its separate explicit human adjudication requirement.

For a positive held-alias query, remove the query's held alias and its declared equivalent/derived forms from every equivalent source copy and all searchable fields, including display-name fallbacks, cached representations and indexes. Preserve at least one allowed reference name for a valid positive target. The view manifest declares exactly which original/derived evidence is withheld; uncontrolled substring deletion is not a substitute for ancestry. If no permitted independent reference representation remains, exclude the case with a reason. Mere removal from one provider row does not create a held-out query.

Direction matters: Latin→Han and Han→Latin require separately validated query/reference views. A family can support both but counts once within each direction and never as two independent families overall. Each direction's denominator is the number of eligible independent final families with reviewed natural evidence and the required retained reference representation. The 100-per-claimed-direction gate cannot be met with 100 aliases from four families. Detailed allocation, exposure controls and scoring restrictions remain in the companion partition plan.

## Immutable release and custody contract

A proposed release manifest binds selected observations, source/terms receipts, grouping and transform versions, review/adjudication decisions, quarantine exclusions, populations and validation results. File-level and logical-content hashes must agree with the declared bytes and rows. Counts include source observations, record-equivalence groups, entity claims, leakage families, natural/derived names and exclusions separately. No count is derived by subtracting 69 unreviewed collision candidates from 600 records.

Build into a new temporary destination, validate it, then make the complete local artifact visible under a previously unused release path such as `data/corpora/research-v1/`. This is a proposed future private path, not an artifact created by Step 08A. A failure must leave prior releases, source selections and active stores unchanged. A release number does not expand its permission, source scope or eligibility.

Exposure events record what population/family or artifact was accessible, to whom, when, for what purpose, and with what evidence. Exposure follows links and derivations across versions. Store only references to final custody attestations in developer-visible artifacts; do not export final examples, labels, per-case hashes that act as identifiers, rankings or slice results for development. Any necessary curation access is recorded separately from developer access. Hashes bind content but do not prove secrecy; encryption with a developer-accessible key does not establish independent custody.

Corpus curation may produce reviewed candidates; Step 09's custodian freezes actual partition/view membership and final study material under the separate access protocol. Before confirmation, developer-facing audits return only approved aggregates and integrity pass/fail results. Detailed failures go to the authorized custodian. No final labels or assignments are included in this planning document.

## Exact invariants for later implementation

1. Every admitted observation has one verified source snapshot and an applicable purpose/field permission decision; a bare URL or availability flag never suffices.
2. Source keys preserve exact provider IDs and namespaces. Observation identity includes the snapshot. Conflicting repeats fail without overwriting prior evidence.
3. Every name and derived string has complete original-field or transformation ancestry. Language, source and identity are never inferred from script alone.
4. Exact text equality can nominate review but cannot merge records, assert identity or make a positive label.
5. Accepted record equivalence, real-world identity claims and leakage-family membership have different types and versioned evidence. Parent/subsidiary links do not imply identity.
6. Unresolved or conflicting links quarantine all affected eligible uses before independence is counted; no unresolved endpoint can become a hard negative by default.
7. Explicitly weak aliases are excluded from supervised labels/mining/final query use until an evidenced purpose-specific resolution. Source flags retain their actual schema meaning.
8. Candidate kind, proposed label, provider assertion, AI suggestion, human submission and adjudicated label are separate fields/states. No automated action increments a human-review count.
9. The initial review minimum is 200 distinct proposed-positive plus 200 distinct negative-candidate cases, with source/script/type stratification and shortage reporting. Accepted counts may be smaller.
10. Every used hard negative has a valid explicit human adjudication, different eligible families under the policy, adequate evidence and no unresolved identity link.
11. A natural nonmember and an all-copies-withheld query carry distinct construction types and fixed view hashes. Unknown membership never becomes absence.
12. Every absent view excludes all equivalent copies; every held-alias view excludes the held evidence/declared forms across all copies and searchable representations. Changed views require revalidation.
13. Review conclusions and grouping changes are append-only versions. A superseded label cannot remain silently active in a new release.
14. A sealed release refuses overwrite, verifies content and lineage, reports excluded counts, and exposes no partially validated result as accepted.
15. Exposure follows the whole dependent family across source refreshes and new IDs. The existing 600 cannot enter fresh calibration, threshold or final pools through relabeling or resampling.
16. Independence and direction coverage count families, not source copies, alias pairs or perturbations. Target acceptance requires at least 7,000 disjoint reviewed families: 5,000 fixed-index references and 2,000 controlled index-absence families excluded across all equivalent copies. Natural background supplements stay separately identified; a family cannot count in both targets.
17. A new cross-population or cross-partition equivalence/dependence discovery invalidates affected frozen membership and requires a new reviewed version; it cannot be patched invisibly.
18. Detailed final labels, examples and model outputs remain within actual custody boundaries. Public planning and developer audit output reveal no final case-level content.

Each slice needs its own concrete files, commands, tests, risks and acceptance explanation under the project's phase rules. No new library is required merely by this design; any proposed dependency needs a separate rationale and permission.

## Design acceptance and handoff

Step 08A is reviewable when the schema separates evidence from claims, the review and absence definitions are explicit, the exposed pool is barred from fresh confirmation, and the future acceptance examples can reject plausible data errors. Passing a design review does not satisfy Step 08's actual corpus, human-review or coverage requirements.

Before Step 08B, settle the exact population and reference-universe definitions; identify real human review capacity, language competence and independent custody; approve the central plan's bounded source/field/byte/time resources; and confirm purpose permissions for the proposed transformations. Current 600-group eligibility and exact-name candidates remain unlabelled until that human work occurs. Where evidence or staffing is insufficient, retain the shortfall rather than inventing a label or guarantee.

**What:** design a traceable reviewed corpus. **Why:** matching experiments need sound labels and independent groups. **When:** after approved three-source feasibility and before any Step 08B acquisition or implementation. **Where:** this local planning document; future private versioned stores. **Who:** publishers govern reuse, the project owner authorizes scope, humans review evidence, and the implementation enforces mechanical invariants. **How:** preserve observations, separate link meanings, quarantine uncertainty, record actual review, bind fixed views and keep releases and exposure history immutable.
