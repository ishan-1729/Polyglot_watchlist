## 2. Label provenance: preserve separate facts

Use two dimensions, not one label that erases history: `evidence_origin` (publisher assertion, independent documentary evidence, synthetic derivation) and `review_origin` (none, single human, independently reviewed humans). Also retain task definition, evidence/version/hash, source/terms, review actor/time/competence, uncertainty, conflicts, exposure and permitted uses.

| Label class | What it supports | What it does not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source-supported | Publisher explicitly associates original names with its record, or supplies permitted verifiable identity evidence | Error-free legal identity, linguistic equivalence, independent human validation |
| Synthetic | A documented fictional/perturbation generation rule and parent lineage | Natural multilingual coverage, extra independent entities or human evidence |
| Single-reviewer | Owner actually inspected cited evidence and recorded a judgment | Independent adjudication or unbiased final validation by the developer |
| Independent human | Actual separate qualified people and their attributable decisions under the original protocol | Never available by declaring the owner twice or using AI agents; currently zero |

Source-supported positives target **retrieval of the publisher-supported record/target set**. Preserve historic/weak/ambiguous alias restrictions; do not silently promote shared-brand or generic names. Corroborating sources may copy the same upstream feed, so count evidence lineage rather than treating two publishers as two independent witnesses. External ground truth is useful only when its provenance, intended target, rights and overlap are verified; none is newly acquired or assumed here. AI suggestions cannot become independent ground truth.

Measure actual review time and insufficiency before expanding. Forty inspected cases do not mean forty accepted labels.

## 4. Corpus construction and absence, without invented identity

1. Reuse the **12,090 organization observations**, separately retaining 1,568 vessels and all 40,568 name entries. Keep the 1,259 possible duplicate edges, 657 components and 2,864 flags. Missing flags do not prove uniqueness or eligibility.
2. Build an auditable eligibility table for every candidate: permitted purpose, complete source/alias ancestry, type, evidence quality, known links, exposure, label class and exclusion reasons. Explicit source-supported alias groups may qualify for the narrow publisher-record task under the proposed weak-supervision policy; acceptance as an independent family additionally needs adequate identity/dependence evidence. Do not call raw source-local rows independent entities.
3. Keep canonical equivalence, identity, leakage dependence and unresolved similarity separate. Supported links may form groups; uncertain links do not merge identities. Exclude unresolved connected components from counted reference/absence cohorts. If retained only for development diagnostics, keep them together as conservative leakage blocks and report their separate status. More generous grouping for leakage prevention is not a label of sameness. Screen additional near-duplicate/dependence evidence; the existing name-equality nominations are not a complete duplicate oracle.
4. Select about 5,000 eligible reference families and 2,000 different eligible absence families by frozen, score-independent rules. Seven thousand from 12,090 rows requires at least **57.90% usable-independent yield**, before other needs; it is unmeasured. Source-local rows, singleton graph nodes and repeat aliases cannot fill a shortfall. A single-name family may serve as an absence target without qualifying for a held-alias positive. Do not force the targets through weak evidence.
5. Define absence **only relative to one fixed complete reference view**. Exclude every supported equivalent copy, all aliases and descendants from that view, indexes, display data, caches and memory. A possible unresolved copy blocks the claim. Resolve homonyms/multiple valid targets or mark ambiguity; do not call every different-family name negative. Record query origin, withheld family, complete-view hash, graph/decision versions and exclusion checks. Revalidate on any membership change using the C3 scientific invariants through a separately approved solo-compatible contract.
6. Report denominators, evidence classes and residual duplicate risk. Documentary insufficiency blocks affected cohorts, not the software. Later authorized evidence supplementation or an explicit quantitative scope decision is required if the 7,000-family target cannot be met. Manual review of 400 sampled cases does not validate every family; automated source-supported eligibility is the explicitly proposed alternative assurance level.

## 5. Evaluation design — specification only, no Step 09 execution

Retain whole-family approximately **50/15/10/10/15 train/development/calibration-fit/threshold-selection/test** allocation, predeclared script/domain strata and seed. Keep both endpoints of supervised pairs/mined negatives inside training. Fit calibration and choose thresholds on separate groups only after the development pipeline freezes. Source snapshots, eligibility, graph, splits, queries and run recipes become immutable versioned manifests.

**All currently retained observations are development-exposed.** This revision must not erase that history or rebrand them as fresh calibration/test. Prospective fresh permitted data remains a separate acquisition need. When later authorized, a self-holdout process should prepare source-supported eligibility mechanically without printing held-out names/answers; log any necessary owner curation as exposure and exclude affected families from fresh confirmation eligibility. Use frozen scripts, restricted output, sealed artifacts, access/run logs and counts-only integrity checks. The owner can bypass local controls, so these are procedural safeguards, not independent custody. Cases whose truth requires manual developer inspection cannot also be described as unobserved by that developer.

Freeze model/code/thresholds/metrics/denominators and authorized run count before one planned internal test evaluation. Access to test answers or performance for tuning makes that cohort development-exposed; a later valid confirmation needs a new cohort. No adaptive resplitting or repeated “final” runs. Report source-supported and solo-reviewed performance separately, synthetic robustness separately, and supplemental/sanctions domains separately. Use group-level paired uncertainty estimates, never treat aliases as independent samples. Such intervals quantify sampling variation conditional on the benchmark and assumed labels; they do not account away systematic label bias, unknown duplicate dependence or researcher exposure.

The Latin–Arabic, Latin–Cyrillic and especially Latin–Han goals remain. Existing **27 Han source-local candidates are exposed, unreviewed development material**, not 27 fresh final successes. At least 100 eligible independent final groups per claimed direction remains unmet. Existing saved metadata can inform a later bounded Wikidata supplemental proposal or official sanctions enrichment proposal; neither is authorized or a guaranteed yield. Supplemental organizations cannot replace fresh sanctions-domain cases. No translations, synthetic pairs or alias multiplication fill natural coverage. If supply or competence is inadequate, report the direction as unsupported and keep its agreed gate open until acquisition succeeds or a separate explicit coverage amendment is approved.

Permissible eventual claim: “Reproducible internal research benchmark against disclosed source-supported/single-reviewer labels, with documented group controls and exposure limitations.” Not permissible: independently validated human ground truth, inter-rater reliability, unbiased independently held confirmation, real-world identity probabilities, operational false-positive guarantees, population prevalence, proven analyst benefit or supported performance on unmet script/domain slices. Group separation can remain valid without multiple reviewers; label/custody independence cannot be claimed. Step 17 calibration must describe the narrower record-support labels; Step 22 remains N=1 descriptive unless separately expanded; Step 28 cannot retain the word “independent” for a solo run. These downstream limitations require explicit documentation, not wholesale completion of those steps now.
